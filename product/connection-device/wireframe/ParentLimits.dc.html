What you don’t see
Printed here so you never have to wonder. None of it is a setting.
Her day. The device is not on unless she is holding it.
Anything word-for-word. No transcript is ever made.
Notes she recorded and kept to herself.
Whether she left something off tonight’s list, or how often she does.
Where she is, who she is with, or what she is doing.
Her mood, scored or flagged. Nothing here judges how she sounded.
Audio is processed on the device and destroyed within two seconds. It never reaches us, so we could not show you any of this if you asked.
A screen with no controls on it. Every competitor buries its limits in a privacy policy; stating them as product copy is the differentiation, and it is also what a child will be shown when they ask what their parent can see.
