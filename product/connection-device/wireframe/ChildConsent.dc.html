How this works
Read this with whoever gave it to you.
1
It only hears you when you hold the button. The rest of the time it is off, and you can see that it is off.
2
After you talk, you pick who hears it. You can also keep it, or throw it away.
3
Every evening it makes a short list of things your grown-up could ask you about. You see the list first and can cross anything off.
4
Nobody is told when you cross something off, or when you decide not to send anything at all.
5
If you want to stop using it, say so. It is yours to switch off.
